## Notes

- `no_ocr` cannot reach `accepted` by contract (a critical check did not run); it measures how much OCR costs, not quality.
- Synthetic fixtures; not customer validation.
- H1 protocol: `designer_ref` plans every size with the second-best hand-written family per orientation (a stand-in for a composition the planner would not pick on its own). `learned` sees exactly one designer_ref variant per aspect class as the approved example (the largest size of that class) and must reproduce the designer's plan on the other sizes of that class. `Agreement` is the mean IoU of planned element boxes against the designer plan on those held-out sizes; it is reported for every constraint-planner config, so `joint`/`full` give the no-example counterfactual and `designer_ref` the ceiling (1.0 by construction). `Mean s` for `learned` includes the setup (render example, match, infer) on the example sizes.
## Reading

`run_ablations.py --configs grammar --sizes 728x90,970x250 --cases 15`, OCR on.

- Before the fix below, 728×90 accepted only 3/15: nine failures were the subject-integrity
  check calling a 22×10 px logo "stretched" (scale 0.122 by 0.125 — one pixel of integer
  rounding on a tiny box, not a distortion) and three were long-copy stacks that do not fit a
  90 px strip. 970×250 accepted 15/15.
- The distortion tolerance now grows with one pixel of rounding on the smaller side of the
  box (`0.02 + 1/min(w, h)`); a genuinely stretched tiny box (30×10 from 180×80) still fails.
  After the fix: 728×90 accepts 12/15 and 970×250 15/15 (table above, 27/30 overall).
- The three remaining 728×90 failures are the long-text fixtures: the headline, subheadline
  and CTA stack cannot fit 90 px tall, the CTA is planned below the canvas and the contract
  fails the render. A strip-aware arrangement (copy beside the CTA instead of above it) is
  the next planner step; until then the verdict is honest.

## Hideable copy (`8891194`)

The three long-copy failures are the stack not fitting 90 px at the minimum sizes. When the
design explicitly allows the subheadline to be left out on small sizes (`allowed.hide`, the
element panel's "May be left out on small sizes"), the planner drops it least-important-first
only after the smallest sizes still overflow, records `dropped:sub:no_room`, and never
touches the headline, CTA, logo or an element under a keep_visible rule. With that permission
the three cases render 728×90 as `accepted` single variants (headline + CTA inside the
canvas); inside a size family the consistency check still flags the missing element for
review, so nothing disappears silently. Without the permission the verdict stays `failed`.
